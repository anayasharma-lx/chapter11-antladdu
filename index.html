Ch. 11 · 3D Shapes & Nets
Ant & Laddu
An ant wants to reach a laddu on a cuboid by the shortest surface path. Unfold the cuboid — the shortest path becomes a straight line on the net.
Scenario
Unfolding
Cuboid — drag to rotate
Net — path as straight line

Unfold the cuboid into a flat net. On a flat surface, the shortest path between two points is always a straight line. Different ways of unfolding give different straight-line lengths — you must check all valid unfoldings to find the true minimum.
